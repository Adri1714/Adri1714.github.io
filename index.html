Background

I started programming as a kid with Scratch, and the habit of taking an idea and turning it into something playable never left. Today I study Video Game Design and Development at CITM (UPC), where I split my time between the technical and creative sides of making games.

Most of what I enjoy lives at the intersection of the two: figuring out how a system should behave, then making it feel good to play. I like breaking problems down step by step until they click, and game jams are where I push hardest — short deadlines, tight constraints, a finished thing at the end.

When I'm not building, I'm usually playing indie games and taking notes on what makes them work.
